How to delete your data

Applies to every Memes for Laughs account, including accounts created with Google or Facebook

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to Memes for Laughs, the same way you usually do.
  2. Open your profile: choose your name at the top right of the page. At the bottom of your profile, choose Delete my account. (If you're asked to accept the Terms first, the same option is at the bottom of that screen.)
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, and choose Delete account.

That's it: you're signed out, and the deletion happens right away.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, password hash, plan and settings
  • Your linked Google and Facebook sign-ins
  • All your meme sets: the topics you typed, the photos you uploaded and the meme images
  • Your favorites, your API keys and your share links (shared links stop working at once)
  • Any open email links (confirmation, password reset)

We can't undo a deletion. If you sign up again later, you start with a new, empty account. Server logs and backups may still contain traces for a limited time, as explained in the Privacy policy.

Can't sign in?

Write to hamid70yazdi@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within one month and confirm by email.

Signed in with Facebook?

Removing Memes for Laughs from your Facebook settings (Settings → Apps and websites) stops Facebook from sharing your data with us, but doesn't delete what we already have. To delete that too, use Delete my account in the app, or write to us as described above.
