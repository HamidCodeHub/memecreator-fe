Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-22 · Applies to the Memes for Laughs website and app

In short
  • We collect only what we need to run your account and make your memes.
  • The topics you type are sent to an AI service to write the captions.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

This policy covers Memes for Laughs (memesforlaughs.com). The data controller is Hamidreza Yazdi, Milan, Italy (full postal address). For any question about your data or to use your rights, write to hamid70yazdi@gmail.com.

To check: whether the service is run as a private person or through a company (then name, VAT number and registered office go here), and whether to use a dedicated address such as privacy@memesforlaughs.com. A Data Protection Officer is probably not required at this size; confirm.

Please don't type personal details about yourself or others (health, contact details, real people's names in hurtful contexts) into topics. Topics are processed by an AI service (see section 4).

Photos you upload

When you choose Use your photo, we send the photo (and your topic, if you wrote one) to our AI service (see section 4), which looks at it to write the captions. We keep the photo and the memes made from it with your account, so you can see them again, until you delete your account. Your photo is never used as a template for other people and is never shown to anyone, unless you share one of its memes. We don't use it to recognize who is in it. Legal basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Only upload photos you're allowed to use. If other people are in the photo, make sure they're fine with it.

To check with a lawyer: whether photos of other people need more wording or a consent step; whether to delete uploads sooner (for example after 30 or 90 days) instead of keeping them until the account is deleted; that Groq's terms cover processing images. If "put your face on a meme" is opened to users: faces may count as biometric data (Art. 9) when used to locate faces; that feature deletes saved faces after 90 days.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads and don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't use your topics or memes to train AI models, and we don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similar effects.
  • We don't load fonts or scripts from other companies' servers: the website is served entirely from our own server.

5. How long we keep data

  • Your account and memes: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, your linked Google/Facebook sign-ins, your memes and their images, your favorites, your API keys and your share links straight away. Shared links stop working at once.
  • Shared memes: stay visible to anyone with the link until you delete your account. Decide whether people should also be able to stop sharing a single meme (not built yet), and whether shared links should expire. Copies other people already saved or posted are outside our control.
  • Unconfirmed sign-ups: deleted automatically 7 days after sign-up if the email was never confirmed.
  • Email links: confirmation links work for 24 hours, password links for 1 hour; expired links are deleted the next night.
  • Sign-in sessions: end when you sign out or after 7 days without activity.
  • Security counters (failed sign-ins, hourly limits): kept in memory for up to an hour, never written to disk.
  • Server logs (which may include IP addresses and email addresses): 30 days — set up log rotation to match.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in backups for up to N days.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your account and data — directly in the app (see How to delete your data) or by email;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format (portability).

Write to hamid70yazdi@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority in the EU country where you live.

8. Age

Memes for Laughs is only for people aged 18 or over, because the jokes can be rude. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a minor has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes; email links and API keys only as SHA-256 hashes. Sign-in sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies. Repeated wrong passwords are slowed down. No system is perfectly secure: if a breach affects your data, we'll inform you and the authority as the law requires.

Before launch: everything served over HTTPS, server backups encrypted, and a short internal note on how to handle a breach (72-hour notification to the Garante).

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll show you the new version when you next sign in and ask you to accept it before you continue. The version date is at the top of this page.
